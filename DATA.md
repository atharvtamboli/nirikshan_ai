## How to reproduce the lookup

Use the tender ID on the [Rajasthan eProcurement portal](https://eproc.rajasthan.gov.in/nicgep/app) if a stored detail link expires. Inspect Basic Details, Work Item Details, Critical Dates and Tender Inviting Authority. For Paner, the linked status page identifies the ID. Use View More Details, or the [indexed tender detail](https://eproc.rajasthan.gov.in/nicgep/app?component=%24DirectLink&page=WebTenderStatus&service=direct&session=T&sp=S2k553paOJimnCa1A1cGy4dkBBlbxTGqaDp95SQPImxU%3D), to locate the estimate. Portal session links and downloads can expire independently of the notice.

Amounts in the seed use integer paise converted from the INR Tender Value field. Publication dates identify the notice. Short English descriptions and Hindi display names are editorial. For Dabayacha a [secondary bid comparison](https://tenderkart.in/tender/d1d6aa17-1d28-4e44-af8b-27153c00de76) identifies a lowest quoted bid. It does not supply a signed award or payment record. A listed schedule of quantities describes the tendering basis. Actual usage requires work-linked measurement evidence.

## Closing the gaps

Ask the issuing division for the signed award or work order, certified running/final bill and referenced measurement book pages, payment voucher and date, and bridge-specific inspection or test sheet. Match the work identity and contract scope before recording a figure. Keep separate dates for the source and the review. An approved evidence entry denotes a moderation decision about a source; material quality and wrongdoing require independent substantiation.
